--- what this proves ---
The v2 prompt's no-op contract is "restate the current instanceClass with no
annotations". That is only safe if an identical server-side apply does not bump
resourceVersion - otherwise every no-op is a write, the write re-triggers the
WatchOperation, and the loop never settles.

12 samples over 4 minutes: resourceVersion frozen at 161746, lastScheduleTime
frozen, exactly 1 model call. Confirmed safe.
